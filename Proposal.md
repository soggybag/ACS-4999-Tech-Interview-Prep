
1. Goals
The goal of this independent study is to develop strong problem-solving skills in Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA) as preparation for technical software engineering interviews. By the end of the 7 weeks, the student will have worked through the NeetCode  problem set, covering core topics including arrays, hashing, two pointers, sliding window, binary search, linked lists, trees, graphs, heaps, backtracking, dynamic programming, and tries. The student will be able to recognize common problem patterns, write correct solutions under timed conditions, and analyze the time and space complexity of their work.
2. Methodology
Intensive weekly instruction and roughly the student completes 4-5 hours per week of homework drilling questions. The primary resources are NeetCode (neetcode.io) for the curated problem list and video explanations, and LeetCode (leetcode.com) as the coding platform. Each week ends with a written deliverable such as complexity annotations, pattern notes, or a template written from memory to reinforce retention beyond just solving problems.
3. Evaluation (to be completed by instructor)
1-on-1 real-time assessment of the student’s progress and mastery. 
Prepared notes on questions and solutions
A final mock interview
4. Reason for Independent Study
Technical interview preparation is not covered as a standalone topic in the Applied Computer Science curriculum, yet it is one of the most critical skills for securing software engineering internships and full-time roles after graduation. This independent study fills that gap in a structured, credit-bearing format. Given the student's goal of applying to graduate programs in AI/ML and pursuing software engineering roles in the near term, developing fluency in DSA problem solving is a direct and immediate priority that warrants dedicated academic focus.
